House rules, learned from labelled examples:
- Model confuses feature requests or general praise/feedback with specific product inquiries due to overlapping language about features. These go to feedback, not product_question.
- Model mistakes shipping-related queries (tracking, delivery) for billing issues when text mentions payment or charges alongside shipping context. These go to shipping, not billing.
- Model incorrectly labels positive feedback about fast shipping or packaging as a shipping issue instead of general feedback. These go to feedback, not shipping.
- Model treats general product capability questions (device compatibility, features) as technical support issues due to mention of devices or platforms. These go to product_question, not technical.
- Model confuses plan upgrade or feature questions with billing when text mentions plan changes or pricing. These go to product_question, not billing.

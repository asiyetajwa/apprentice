House rules, learned from labelled examples:
- Model confuses feature requests or general praise (feedback) with specific product inquiries due to overlapping language about features. These go to feedback, not product_question.
- Model incorrectly assigns billing queries to shipping when text includes both payment and delivery terms, failing to prioritize intent. These go to billing, not shipping.
- Model mislabels shipping-related issues as billing when text mentions payment or charges alongside delivery, due to weak contextual disambiguation. These go to shipping, not billing.

Ticket: The search speed improvement in v3.2 is noticeable, great work on that optimization.
Label: feedback

Ticket: The starter kit arrived quickly and the packaging was lovely, but I still need a corrected receipt for the charge on my credit card.
